A finding from a real-style report

High priority · Quick win

Your headline says "Welcome" instead of what you do

What we saw: The only headline on the page is "Welcome to our website."
Why it matters: Visitors decide in seconds whether they're in the right place. A generic headline makes them hit Back.
Do this: Replace it with your outcome and location, e.g. "Same-day plumbing repairs in Austin, upfront prices." Put a "Book a plumber" button directly underneath.
